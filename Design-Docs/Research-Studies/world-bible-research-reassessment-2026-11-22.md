## 1. Revised decision lens

The [World Bible-first direction](../World-Bible-UX-Direction-2026-11-22.md) makes the encyclopedia the working product: entry directory, author article and semantic component editing, contextual connections, hierarchies, evidence, history and an authorized reader. Distinguish stable canonical identity and typed facts from versioned Canvas presentation and compiled Display. The three underlying representations remain. A node without an article is a valid author record. One shared world/date/revision/branch/perspective context with explicit capabilities; no hidden substitution of main or author mode. Prefer a simple authoring flow over exposing architecture as compulsory studios.

Assess each proposal against (1) author task completion and cognitive load, (2) component reuse and stable identity, (3) exact historical/provenance and scope integrity, (4) existing implementation reuse and cost, (5) comparative evidence and stop conditions. A capability that is technically feasible may still be low priority or unnecessary. New semantics require the existing change-governance process; this review cannot widen accepted scope.

### Evidence limitations and negative results

- #65 explicitly records the initial unforced recursive query timing out and later indexed query success; bounded sparse tree results do not establish dense historical DAG performance. Full authorization must precede traversal and aggregation. No relationship becomes transitive just because a tree can display it.
- #66 follow-up needed coordinate normalization for JSON replay and a precision grid before shared-boundary intersection passed in its limited fixture. No general topology proof. At 50k simple rectangles, SVG serialization 85.8 ms versus DOM insertion/layout 352.4 ms and Canvas2D draw+readback 106.5 ms are different operations, not comparable frame times. Prefer viewport/LOD/labels before acceleration.
- #69 joins and chains return different information: terminal-only SQL vs evidence trace. No claim of transform speedup or actual author time saved.
- #71 metadata-only novel category feasibility is not proof that curated/fixed validators are inferior. Keep the actual A/B/C comparator and do not use a rich taxonomy as a mandate for new fields.
- #76 article grouping needs authorized compilation before counting. Mixed-corpus BM25 can expose ordering changes caused by hidden records; this is prospective risk, not a demonstrated current Atlas leak. Template lifecycle model highlights literal vs self-binding and shallow nested filter rebinding, not proof of production repair. Synthetic embedding counts hidden targets toward work budget; availability noninterference unproven. Projection and lifecycle experiments lack production history, policy and real editor/runtime. Prior recorded results unchanged.

## 4. Cross-domain implications, beyond the research tickets

[04](../04-Genealogy-Relations-and-Genetics.md) and [lineage ADR](../ADRs/cross-phase-lineage-discovery-genetics.md) distinguish biological/legal parentage, household and dynasty; synthetic inheritance remains optional and distinct from culture. Family card/tree can use the same Person ref; ancestry derived, not stored as duplicate sibling/cousin facts. [Network ADR](../ADRs/cross-phase-network-discovery.md) already provides sourced network facts and author-only NetworkTable/ImpactTable; do not expose those unrestricted projections directly to a reader. The new editor should reuse actual services, not conflate membership with constituent hierarchy, jurisdiction or command authority. Use domain-specific hierarchy lenses and typed direct relationships; cross-lens navigation selects the same record but never composes truth through unrelated relationships. A house, military unit and building may all link to the same person without one canonical folder parent.

[Phase-zero verification](../Phase-0-Verification.md) and archived graph captures remain relevant for contextual graph tools and accessible/no-GPU behavior, not proof of a good article editor. No new renderer or storage architecture needed. The article surface is a responsive document layout, not a shader/node editor.

### Prototype critique and next comparative research tasks

The existing [World Bible HTML prototype](world-bible-prototype/index.html) demonstrates an isolated shell, local mock relation picker, identity-only creation, sample reader filter and direct related-entry grouping. It does not implement a real hierarchical traversal/graph, canonical command validation, live typed Canvas components, rich-text styling, historical context or dirty draft guard. Do not treat the prototype as a complete product or security test. Its Playwright task test is pending verification at the recorded baseline. Do not change production solely to match its appearance.

Compare current integrated native workbench A against a minimal entry-centered shell B with identical fixture, domain services, context controls and task completion criteria. Do not handicap A or give B mock capabilities that A must validate. Only test B after real integration; a third option (extra mandatory node-builder C) is unnecessary unless a demonstrated task cannot fit A/B. Use Northbridge plus validated larger mixed corpus and scoped cases, actual actions, context switches, time to first useful result, validation/recovery errors, keyboard accessibility and visible status. Capture real screenshots under UTC convention; labels must distinguish isolated browser, mock-native and native runtime. No new captures generated by this desk review.

## 7. Decision recommendation

Keep the research conclusions that protect semantic integrity and portability. Reweight product priorities toward author experience, reuse existing canonical storage and domain/query/compiler services, retire the exploratory recommendation of obligatory separate studio navigation. Keep the optional graph as a genuine multi-entity work surface. This review favors simpler UI integration, not simplifying away historical or policy invariants.
