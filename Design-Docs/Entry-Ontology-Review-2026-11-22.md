## Executive assessment

Atlas has a strong temporal relational foundation, particularly genealogy and institutional/event/conflict domains, but is not yet a broad worldbuilding ontology. The principal gap is structured representation of cultures, normative ideas, knowledge/language, species, technologies and material systems. Many should not be new Rust enum variants. Distinguish independent identity, typed fact, definition/classification, instance, event/process, evidence, assertion/knowledge, derived projection and presentation. First finish original commitments and accepted #68; then assess genuinely new categories with owner and formal research/feature lifecycle. Do not make #70 absorb missing ontological semantics.

This is a useful substrate, not implemented rich subtype ontology. The other seven variants include events, source/claim, definition and reified records. ObjectRef is actually (world,id), runtime resolves kind; logical design pseudotype includes kind. Preserve stable IDs and verify declared kind at boundaries, don't blindly change references.

## 2. Ontology boundaries and semantic drift

[03 §1/5–9](03-Domain-and-Temporal-Model.md), [04](04-Genealogy-Relations-and-Genetics.md), [05](05-Events-Actors-and-Conflicts.md), [06](06-Three-Layer-Entries-and-Canvas.md), [01 requirements](01-Requirements-and-Invariants.md) govern. Findings:
- **Existing-scope gap:** Place and Concept appear in logical registry but not compiled main. Place is already in approved #68; #67 draft not merged. Concept belongs to original 03/06; define baseline meaning and limits under original phases, not a new feature just for this name. Specific structured culture/language/etc mechanisms may exceed original scope.
- **Existing-scope gap:** Relationship endpoints currently both require_person; EventInvolvement requires_person(participant); ActorAction requires_person(actor), target any visible object ([store.rs L3280–3352](https://github.com/sdhaight1-hash/ConWorld-Tools/blob/47938bd4ac4a68e97b265dea53350d16757fad6f/crates/atlas-store/src/store.rs#L3280-L3352)). [REL-01/ORG-02/EVT-02](01-Requirements-and-Invariants.md) call for compatible arbitrary object pairs, group-group relationships, organizations as participants. Network membership is Person→Organization/Household/FamilyHouse/Faction; not group→group. OfficeHolding/TitleHolding Person only; Ownership Person/institution→Asset; ConflictPosition Faction→Conflict/ConflictIssue. This is partial implementation, not a reason for duplicative entry types. Each relationship must have explicit endpoint/role/cardinality/time validation, not arbitrary all-to-all.
- **Existing-scope gap:** Definitions are addressable, but relationship kind is a string, tags inline strings; not all governed endpoint/temporal tag contracts are implemented. Distinguish vocabulary definition from a real-world concept (e.g. relationship label “follower” vs doctrine or specific tradition).
- **Integrity design gap:** network facts (18 kinds: membership, marriage, succession, confirmed/alleged cause, conflict position, office holding/of, title holding/of, ownership, issue/phase/rule of, population member, heir designation, identity redirect, alliance, settlement) live as stable UUID records in dedicated network_facts table not universal registry. Accepted parentage likewise separate stable record. Logical model wants membership and relationships annotatable/linkable/effect targets. Need a unified authorized fact-reference contract (discriminated object/fact ref or justified registry reification), preserve IDs; do not duplicate fact rows as fake objects. Claims require actual registry subject today, so can't assume arbitrary fact UUID is claim/Canvas target. Review source/actual schemas before change. Source: [network structs](https://github.com/sdhaight1-hash/ConWorld-Tools/blob/47938bd4ac4a68e97b265dea53350d16757fad6f/crates/atlas-store/src/network.rs#L159-L190), [record_network_fact](https://github.com/sdhaight1-hash/ConWorld-Tools/blob/47938bd4ac4a68e97b265dea53350d16757fad6f/crates/atlas-store/src/store/network.rs#L82-L250), [family store](https://github.com/sdhaight1-hash/ConWorld-Tools/blob/47938bd4ac4a68e97b265dea53350d16757fad6f/crates/atlas-store/src/store/family.rs), [Claim validation](https://github.com/sdhaight1-hash/ConWorld-Tools/blob/47938bd4ac4a68e97b265dea53350d16757fad6f/crates/atlas-store/src/store.rs#L3352-L3380).
- **Existing-scope gap:** belief/knowledge, attributed evidence and causal effects must remain distinct from claim and publication visibility. Claim statement/audience is not complete belief model, no automatic knowledge from fact access. HistoricalEffect and TagAssignment in logical designs do not require standalone editor entries by default. Implement addressability/structured relation only as existing design requires.
- **Geography:** #68 [13](13-Spatial-and-Nested-Location-Model.md) independent Place types and optional geometry, role/lens facts. A polity's government is Organization, jurisdiction is temporal authority over Place, territory is Place, claim is Claim. City Place vs municipal Organization vs residents/Population. Ship Asset vs interior Place and its location. Do not encode ownership/authority via containment. No duplicate Country/City/Building core kind for ordinary geography.
- **Presentation:** nine actual built-in templates (blank, character, family, organization, event, conflict, location, genealogy, source) [layout.rs L67–77](https://github.com/sdhaight1-hash/ConWorld-Tools/blob/47938bd4ac4a68e97b265dea53350d16757fad6f/crates/atlas-store/src/layout.rs#L67-L77), [canvas-templates.ts](https://github.com/sdhaight1-hash/ConWorld-Tools/blob/47938bd4ac4a68e97b265dea53350d16757fad6f/apps/desktop/src/canvas-templates.ts). Location template not Place creation; genealogy report is projection, blank a layout, character template not second Person. Custom templates customize layout, not domain typing. General editor accepts resolved object registry ref; current entry layout storage main-branch only ([entries.rs L9–70,425–475](https://github.com/sdhaight1-hash/ConWorld-Tools/blob/47938bd4ac4a68e97b265dea53350d16757fad6f/crates/atlas-store/src/store/entries.rs#L425-L475)). #4/#5 must not claim all kinds have full native author/read support or forks because kind filter exists.
- **Discovery and transforms:** ghost proposal is draft separate from canonical registry; stable typed review required. #70 operation availability must reflect verified underlying semantics, not allow new types automatically. Recipe definition/MapSpec/layout/saved query/graph overlay are author/presentation metadata; not in-world entries.

## 3. Candidate worldbuilding taxonomy (not approved)

Aim for small typed core, controlled world-specific subtype and qualifying-role vocabulary, versioned typed attributes, facts and sources. Generic Concept for named referenceable knowledge/categories without specialized invariants; avoid EAV as whole domain per 03. Allow identity to have simultaneous contextual classifications and type evolution only under explicit reviewed semantics; do not change core kind or ID merely due to rename or changed role. Prefer separate identities only when lifecycle, independent relations/history or rules demand it.

| Domain / family | Examples | Minimal representation recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| Individuals / peoples | person, sapient nonhuman, named creature, species, lineage | Person for supported individual agents; examine broader fictional species/organism kind or subtype/ruleset only if distinct genealogy/life-cycle constraints needed. Species vs individual vs population separate; not implicit human biology. |
| Kinship and society | clan, dynasty, tribe, family, household, caste, class, citizenship | Existing FamilyHouse/Household/Organization for actual organized entities; collective identity/classification may be Concept or new reviewed social-group type if membership rules genuinely differ; not Population as proxy for culture. Roles/status sourced temporal facts. |
| Political and institutional | state, kingdom, republic, council, guild, university, company, army, churches, orders | Organization subtype, Faction when independent alignment; place/territory and government separate. Office, Title and authority contracts already exist. Polity subtype vs new kind only after actual governance constraints reviewed. |
| Cultural and linguistic | culture, ethnicity, language, dialect, writing system, tradition, custom, holiday, artistic school | Concept subtypes as first referenceable descriptions; a festival occurrence is HistoricalEvent, recurring observance concept/definition; adoption/practice/language use/versioned cultural relations separately. Structured languages/cultural evolution may justify new schema only when concrete queries/constraints warrant. |
| Religion and philosophy | faith, pantheon, doctrine, ideology, myth, ritual, deity | Concept for beliefs/doctrines/stories, Organization for institutions, Claim for testimony, belief/adherence typed facts; in-world deity as actual agent only if author's canon explicitly establishes it. Belief not fact; myth and historical event not automatically same. |
| Geography / environment | planet, continent, biome, region, river, sea, country, city, district, building, room, habitat | #68 Place subtypes/lenses; environmental classifications/condition values as appropriate; planet geometry and climate simulation beyond bounded #68, don't automatically add terrain engine. |
| Law and governance | law, constitution, treaty, charter, contract, succession doctrine, rights, prohibition | Source for text/evidence, HistoricalEvent for adoption/signing, Concept for named legal ideas; investigate Rule/Agreement identity if obligations, parties, jurisdiction, validity and amendments are independently queryable. SuccessionRule remains specialized. |
| Material and economy | individual sword, fleet ship, estate, resource deposit, commodity, currency, profession, trade route, debt, transaction | Asset instance vs product/material/currency concept or type; profession role/Concept; physical route Place/geometry as approved or typed route connection; transfer event plus sourced ownership facts; contract/debt as reified multi-party obligation only if needed. Structured measures/units, trade ledgers and monetary systems distinct potential scope. Don't make every item category a core kind. |
| Technology and knowledge | technology, invention, craft, technique, scientific theory, spell, magic system, skill, recipe | Concept subtypes and sourced versioned adoption/knowledge links; invention discovery is event; actual artifact Asset. Rules for mechanics or simulation separate from lore descriptions and #70 workflows. |
| Life and ecology | species, ecosystem, disease, pathogen, crop, domestic animal, resources, climate | Concept taxonomy, Place habitat, Population reference with explicit semantics, independent organism identity only if gameplay/narrative needs; disease outbreak Event vs disease type Concept. Ecology/species simulation outside existing design. |
| Text, media and culture objects | book/work, edition, letter, decree, artwork, song, inscription, map | Source when evidentiary; Asset for owned physical copy, Concept or distinct work identity where content/revision/creator and copies need independent identity. | Epistemic and authoring | testimony, rumor, allegation, secret, belief, alternate account, missing lore | Claim, Source, typed knowledge/belief acquisition/access and epistemic status; draft proposal noncanon. Priority candidate: original-scope Concept with documented classification, world-scoped subtype vocabulary and safe controlled fields (evaluate original 03 definition/extension guarantee before enlarging). For richer structured independent types, shortlist **Culture/social collective, Language, Species, Legal Instrument/Agreement, Work**, and **Resource/Commodity/Technology** where actual lifecycle/relations/validation necessitate beyond Concept. No decision that all qualify; each must clear identity/lifecycle/semantic invariant/query utility test. Do not invent definitive numbers or universal classifications for worlds. Choose type based on intended author usage and explicit facts.

## 4. Decision rules for an entry type

1. Does it have independent identity through rename, place changes, membership or classification changes? If not, value/attribute/tag/role.
2. Is it a concrete instance or an abstract classification/idea? Link instance to controlled type; avoid duplicated identity and unstructured string join.
3. Is it a relation with parties, interval, source and status? Reify a fact when independent annotation/revision/evidence needed, rather than create duplicate entity; n-ary event/contract when domain invariants require.
4. Is it an occurrence or ongoing contested process? HistoricalEvent/subevent vs Conflict/phase; not the same thing as period label.
5. Is it an account of truth or evidence? Claim/Source/knowledge model; do not promote to fact.
6. Is it view content, recipe or generated suggestion? Presentation/derived metadata/draft, not canon.
7. Does a domain require special validation, identity, behavior or queries that the existing typed service cannot express? Only then propose a new core kind/typed dedicated payload. If not, use existing kind + versioned controlled subtype.

Avoid hardcoded per-subtype switches across DB/UI/query/discovery/templates/transform registries. Derive or test capability parity from a single trusted kind/descriptor contract when implementation justified; no general user code, arbitrary fields or second graph engine. Maintain distinction between createable, queryable, linkable, annotatable, publishable, template-compatible and transform-eligible; these capabilities are not implied by shared kind. Use explicit "unclassified/unknown" or unresolved draft instead of default Person, incorrect parent or forced exact date. Source labels/aliases versioned; no dedupe by title alone. Reclassification within compatible subtype preserves identity; core-kind correction requires explicit reviewed migration/redirect and impact evaluation, not silent retype.

## 5. Example stress scenarios and validation

- Kingdom Old Vale: territory Place, crown government Organization, royal House, sovereign Person, monarch Office/Title, charter Source plus law rule/concept, contested claim Declared Claim, coronation Event. Transfer title doesn't move land; new government doesn't automatically erase territory.
- Temple of the Dawn: place building, organization, faith Concept, doctrine Concept, historic manuscript Source, priest Office/role, followers via appropriate sourced relations, festival event. Faith can persist after temple or organization dissolves; a character's false belief and author canon differ.
- River city trade: city Place; council Organization; merchant guild Organization; ship Asset, cabin Place; copper commodity Concept, particular cargo Asset if independently tracked; transaction Event, ownership fact; no automatic guild membership by living nearby.
- Inventor's manuscript: technology Concept, invention event, manuscript as Source, physical copy Asset if needed, claimed inventor Claim; competing sources and unknown date retained.
- Nonhuman species in fantasy: species concept/classification distinct from named sapient Person/agent, population sampling, culture and legal personhood. Alternate genealogy/reproduction rules explicit, don't silently run human genetic defaults.

Tests to prioritize within existing scope: registry/SQL/query/creation/ghost/UI parity and wrong-world/kind; label rename stable references and subtype change if supported; restore and old-schema migration; Person vs group participation and legitimate endpoint restrictions; stable fact/claim ref addressability, branch date/correction/source ambiguity; hidden type/alias/source counts and reader parity; qualified places and assets, #68 SP gates; #70 typed workflow availability from verified services, no automatic canon promotion. Test each capability separately, not just enum existence. No current AT/SP/WT gate passed in this review.
